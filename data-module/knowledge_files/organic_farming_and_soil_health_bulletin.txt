National Centre for Organic and Natural Farming (NCONF) Bulletin
Document: NCONF-SOIL-HEALTH-GUIDELINES-2025
Source Organization: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Govt of India

1. Principles of Natural and Organic Farming:
Organic farming eliminates synthetic chemical pesticides and synthetic fertilizers, replacing them with on-farm biological inputs, crop rotations, green manuring, and biological diversity.

2. Preparation and Application of Jeevamrutha:
- Ingredients for 200 Litres: 10 kg fresh indigenous cow dung, 10 litres cow urine, 2 kg jaggery, 2 kg pulse flour (besan), and a handful of fertile undisturbed farm soil.
- Preparation: Mix in 200 litres of water in a shade-covered drum. Stir clockwise twice daily for 48 hours. Fermentation completes in 4 to 7 days.
- Application: Apply 200 litres per acre through irrigation water or as foliar spray every 15-21 days. Multiplies beneficial soil microbes and accelerates nutrient solubilization.

3. Preparation and Application of Panchagavya:
- Ingredients: Cow dung (5 kg), cow ghee (1 kg), cow urine (3 L), cow milk (2 L), cow curd (2 L), tender coconut water (3 L), sugarcane juice (3 L), and ripe banana paste (1 kg).
- Application: 3% foliar spray (30 ml per litre of water) at 15-day intervals during vegetative and flowering phases. Acts as an organic growth promoter and systemic immunity booster.

4. Vermicomposting Standards:
- Earthworm species: Eisenia fetida or Eudrilus eugeniae.
- Application rate: Apply 2 to 3 tonnes per acre for field crops, 5 tonnes per acre for horticultural crops.
- Nutrient content: Provides 1.5-2.0% Nitrogen, 1.0-1.5% Phosphorus, and 1.5-2.0% Potassium in readily available form.

5. Bio-control Agents for Soil Health:
- Trichoderma viride and Pseudomonas fluorescens @ 2.5 kg/ha mixed with 500 kg FYM controls root rot, wilt, and damping-off.
- Neem cake application @ 250 kg/ha controls plant parasitic nematodes and enriches soil organic carbon.
